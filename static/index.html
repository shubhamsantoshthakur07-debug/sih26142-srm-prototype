🔢 Spectral Band Physics in Remote Sensing

Band 2: Blue (490 nm)

Penetrates atmospheric haze and clear water depths. Identifies water depth variation.

Band 3: Green (560 nm)

Peak chlorophyll reflectance. Accurately maps healthy vegetation cover and agricultural vitality.

Band 4: Red (665 nm)

Maximum chlorophyll absorption. Provides sharp contrast for concrete structures, soil, and roads.

Band 8: Near-Infrared / NIR (842 nm)

Critical defense intelligence band: living plants reflect massive NIR, artificial camouflage nets absorb NIR, and water turns jet black.

Multi-Task Optimization Objective:

L_total = λ1 * L_L1 + λ2 * L_CE + λ3 * L_PSF + λ4 * L_SAM

Simultaneously minimizes spatial reconstruction loss, cross-entropy thematic classification loss, Point Spread Function cycle loss, and Spectral Angle Mapper distortion.

Problem Statement SIH26142 Overview

National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) • Space Technology Theme

🎯 The Strategic Challenge

Title: Deep Learning Based Super Resolution Mapping (SRM) from Medium Resolution Satellite Imageries.

Sponsor: National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO), Prime Minister's Office, Government of India.

Background: Strategic monitoring across India's borders and maritime zones requires timely satellite imagery. While high-resolution (HR) satellites (0.3m–1m) have narrow swaths and long revisit times, medium-resolution satellites (10m–30m Sentinel-2, Landsat) provide free global coverage every 5 days. However, tactical targets are trapped inside mixed pixels.

Objective: Develop an AI solution to perform 4x Super Resolution Mapping, resolving mixed pixels into discrete high-resolution thematic masks and clear imagery without hallucinating false artifacts.

🗸 NTRO Evaluation Checklist Compliance

✔
Multi-Spectral Adaptation: Ingests Blue, Green, Red, and Near-Infrared (Band 8) channels to preserve material identity.
✔
Sub-Pixel Mapping (SRM): Unmixes mixed pixels and allocates sub-pixels to discrete land-cover classes (Water, Veg, Facilities, Roads).
✔
Zero-Hallucination Proof: Sensor PSF cycle-consistency check verifies reconstruction error < 0.015, rejecting generative hallucinations.
✔
Defense Operational Usability: Exportable GeoJSON vectors for QGIS/ArcGIS, multi-page intelligence reporting, and mobile responsiveness.
